10.1 — Scroll: Reading progress bar fills with depth

The Long Article. Scroll inside this box and watch the bar fill.

Section one: an introduction long enough to make the point that reading depth can be measured from scroll metrics alone.

Section two:the middle of the piece, where the bar should read roughly half full once the scrollbar reaches the middle of its travel.

Section three: more body text, keeping the reader scrolling toward the end of the document.

Section four: almost there. The bar continues to track the fraction of the article that has been scrolled through.

Section five:the conclusion. When the bottom of the article is reached, the bar reads exactly one hundred percent.
